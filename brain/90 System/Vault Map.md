# Vault Map

- `00 Inbox` — temporary unclassified capture.
- `10 Projects` — finite outcomes and context, not execution state.
- `20 Areas` — ongoing responsibilities and standards.
- `30 Resources` — source-grounded operational reference.
- `40 Daily` — dated observations and decisions.
- `50 Concepts` — reusable mental models.
- `60 Sources` — provenance and license register.
- `90 System` — templates and curation rules.

The numbered folders provide stable ordering without depending on a plugin.
Use [[Home]] for topic navigation rather than browsing folders alone.
