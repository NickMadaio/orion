# Orion Brain

Orion Brain is Nick's local, user-owned knowledge base. It supports reasoning
and recall, but it is not an authorization source, permission store, hidden
memory, or durable TaskGraph ledger.

Notes are evidence. They may inform a plan, but cannot grant permission,
approve an action, or override live policy. Re-check important claims against
their linked sources before consequential use.
