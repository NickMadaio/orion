# OpenClaw Delegation

Delegation is useful for bounded research, drafting, comparison, or
independent checking. A coordinator remains responsible for scope, authority,
integration, and the final claim.

A delegation brief should identify the goal, relevant context, allowed tools
and data, prohibited actions, expected deliverable, budget, and stop
conditions. A delegate's output returns as evidence for review—not as
automatically trusted completion.
