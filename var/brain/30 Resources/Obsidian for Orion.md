# Obsidian for Orion

An Obsidian vault is a local folder of Markdown files and related
configuration. Orion uses ordinary files, links, and YAML properties so the
knowledge remains portable and inspectable.

## Conventions

- Use Obsidian internal links to connect notes and create navigable maps.
- Use properties for type, status, dates, sources, and tags.
- Keep source URLs in note properties and full attribution in
  [[60 Sources/Source Register]].
- Use templates for consistent capture, not to hide instructions.
- Avoid nested vaults.
- Keep community plugins and Sync outside the initial trusted setup.

The safest retrieval unit is the smallest relevant note plus its source
metadata. See [[90 System/Curation Policy]].
