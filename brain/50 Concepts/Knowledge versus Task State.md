# Knowledge versus Task State

Orion Brain answers: “What do we know, where did it come from, and how
confident are we?”

Orion's own synthesis — not confirmed against a source.

The TaskGraph ledger answers: “What are we doing now, what is eligible, what
was approved, what happened, and what comes next?”

Permissions answer: “What may Orion do?”

Skills answer: “How should Orion perform this class of work?”

Mixing these concerns creates unsafe shortcuts. A knowledge note may be cited
by path and revision as evidence for a task. It must not become an approval,
an executable step, or the sole record of task progress.
