Orion — Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 August 2026

Orion is a personal assistant that runs on one Mac, for one person. It has no server, no accounts, and no other users. Nothing it reads is transmitted to the developer, because there is nowhere for it to be transmitted to.

Who this covers

Orion is built and used by Nick Madaio as a personal tool. It is not distributed, sold, or offered to anyone else. If you are reading this, it is almost certainly because a service asked for a privacy policy URL before granting the app access to its owner's own data.

What Orion reads

When connected to a service, Orion reads only what it needs to display:

  • WHOOP — recovery score, sleep performance and duration, day strain, heart rate variability, and resting heart rate.
  • Apple Reminders — a single list named "Orion", which the app creates. Other lists are never read or written.
  • Apple Calendar — today's events, read only. Orion never creates, edits, or deletes an event.

Every connection above is read-only. Orion does not post, publish, message, purchase, or transfer anything on any connected account.

Where it goes

Nowhere. Data fetched from a connected service is held in memory to draw the dashboard and is not written to any remote system. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party processor. Nothing is sold or shared, because nothing leaves the machine.

Access tokens

Tokens granted by a connected service are stored in the macOS Keychain on the one Mac that Orion runs on. They are encrypted at rest, unlocked with the login session, and marked this device only, so they are never synced to iCloud or to any other computer.

Revoking access

Disconnecting inside Orion deletes the stored tokens from the Keychain immediately. Access can also be revoked from the provider's own settings at any time — WHOOP under Account › Connected Apps — which invalidates the token regardless of what Orion does.

Retention

Orion keeps no history of fetched data. Each refresh replaces the previous values. Deleting the app removes everything it held, and clearing the Keychain entries removes the tokens.
