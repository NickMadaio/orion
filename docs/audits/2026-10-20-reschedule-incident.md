## Root causes

1. **Nothing owned this sentence.** "Reschedule my day after X, put Y first after I get home" has an order, an anchor and a trip home, all in his words and
   his day. No code read them: the 9B form was asked (22 s), failed, and the worker improvised.
2. **A hook with no budget inside a host with a 15 s limit.** `before_prompt_build` did a model call (up to 25 s) and a correction call (30 s). The host gave up at 15 s and
   **silently swapped the 3.9k-token slim prompt for the 17.7k-token full one**. That is the whole token story (below). No error was logged anywhere.
3. **Stacked timeouts with no total.** Desk 8 s, hook 15 s (host), form 30 s, retry 30 s, worker 3 min, the app's 300 s: nothing said "this turn has been
   going for 40 s, stop".
4. **The row.** The shadow log was written only in `before_agent_finalize`: one row, 4 min 40 s after the turn began, and none at all for a turn that errors,
   aborts or is timed out by the host. The trace had no line for `before_prompt_build` or `agent_end`, so the 15 s cut-off was invisible.

**Correction to the brief:** there is a decision row for this turn (runId `7034aae9…`, written 15:20:39.148: `decision {move: escalate, fallback: timeout, ms: 8013}`,
`flow {fallback, "no steps"}`, `reads ["day"]`, `tools 2`, `turnMs 279640`). It sorts last and carries the turn's end time, so a search around 15:16 does not find it.

## What the Mac app shows meanwhile

`RunProgressModel`: "Thinking…" at once, then a line per tool the audit log sees, and after 8 s of nothing "Still working — nothing new for Ns." until the app's 300 s `agentTimeout`
(`RunTimeouts.swift`). It cannot see plugin work (the desk, the form, a prefill): during this turn it showed a counter, not what ORION was waiting on. OpenClaw's plugin API has no progress
or interim-message call (hooks return a prompt or one reply), so there is no way for the plugin to say "still working" without the app changing; the honest fail-fast line above is what it can do.
